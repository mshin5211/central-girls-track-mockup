NEWS & ROSTER UPDATES

Stay informed about our latest track meets, announcements, and events.

SEPTEMBER 26, 2026

EXCEPTIONAL PERFORMANCE AT SATURDAY'S INVITATIONAL

The Girls Track Team showed immense spirit at the latest meet! Coach Hood reported top times across the board in sprinting, with several personal bests broken. Thank you to the families who volunteered for concessions!

SEPTEMBER 15, 2026

FALL CAR WASH FUNDRAISER A HUGE SUCCESS

Thanks to the community's amazing support, our annual car wash raised over $1,350! These funds will go directly to purchasing much-needed pit equipment. Our online fundraiser is also currently at $3,200.

AUGUST 1, 2026

BOOSTERS RECEIVE 501(C)(3) DESIGNATION

We are proud to announce that the Central Girls Track Boosters is now formally recognized as a 501(c)(3) tax-exempt organization. Your donations are now fully tax-deductible!
