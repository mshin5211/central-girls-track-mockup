SUPPORT FROM THE SIDELINES

Volunteers Needed

Because our home meets rely entirely on volunteers, we need you! We are actively looking for local college organizations, running clubs, and community members to help with race timing, field event measuring, and crowd control. Help us make meet-day a success.

Food Trucks Welcome

We want to turn standard meets into vibrant community events! We invite local food trucks to park at our meets in exchange for a vendor fee or a percentage of sales. If you own a food truck, reach out to partner with us.
