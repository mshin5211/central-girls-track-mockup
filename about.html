ABOUT THE BOOSTERS

Dedicated to empowering the Central High School Girls Track Team since 1976.

OUR MISSION

The Booster Club for Champaign Central High School Girls Track is officially recognized as a 501(c)(3) non-profit organization. Our primary focus is to help raise funds for the Central girls track team to ensure every athlete has what they need to succeed.

While the club collects a modest $35 membership fee per family, we must make up a significant difference from fundraising efforts. With pending spending needs for uniforms, team recognition, banquets, and equipment, our work is more vital than ever.
