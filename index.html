Our Mission & Impact

The Central Girls Track Boosters is a 501(c)(3) organization dedicated to bridging the funding gap so every athlete can compete, regardless of their background.

Team Building & Recognition

We fund essential recognition items, end-of-year banquets, state competition shirts, and team meals to foster camaraderie and celebrate their hard work.

Uniforms & Equipment

With an ongoing need for new warm-up apparel, hurdle carts, and pit equipment, we ensure our athletes have the gear needed to compete safely and proudly.

Community Driven

Since our field is not on campus, home meets and fundraisers rely entirely on family and community volunteers. Your support keeps us running.
